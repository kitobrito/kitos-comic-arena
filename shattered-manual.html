The goal

Each Hero starts with 30 Health. Bring the enemy Hero to 0 and you win. You fight with minions on the board, spells, traps and, for some realms, Hero weapons.

Turn order

  1. Opening hands. The first player draws 3 cards, the second 4 plus a choice of Mana Potion or Health Potion. Each player may swap any opening cards once (the mulligan).
  2. Start of turn. You gain a Mana Crystal, refill your Mana and draw a card.
  3. Your actions, in any order: play cards, attack with minions that are ready, and use your Hero Spell (1 use per turn).
  4. End your turn. End-of-turn effects happen (Burn, Bleed and the like). Online, a turn lasts up to 75 seconds.

Mana

You gain 1 Mana Crystal each turn, up to 10. Each card costs the Mana shown in its blue gem. Unspent Mana doesn't carry over. Some realms bend this: Dinosaurs grow faster and get Primal Mana that only pays for Dinosaur cards.

Cards

Minions go on the board (up to 7 each) and attack once per turn, starting the turn after they arrive unless they have Charge or Rush. Their Attack is the gold gem, their Health the red one. Damage stays until healed.

Spells happen once and are gone. Traps wait face down until their trigger. Hero weapons give your Hero Attack and use 1 Durability per swing.

Arrival effects happen when a card is played, Exit effects when it dies. Your hand holds up to 10 cards.

Heroes & fusion

Every realm has a Hero and a Hero Spell. Pair two realms and their heroes fuse into one, with both realms' passives. Some Hero Spells are passive rules for your whole deck (Acidic's Dissolve rule, Monster Hunter's Dual-Wield).

Decks

The main mode is two realms: choose exactly 40 cards from both realms' pools, at least 10 from each, no more than 2 copies of a card. Each realm brings a fixed 30-card pool plus any expansion cards you've unlocked. The recommended cut builds a solid deck for you.

One-realm decks are 30 cards from that realm.

Ranked rules

IronBronzeSilverGoldPlatinumDiamondMasterLegendary
  • Iron to Diamond have five divisions each (5 is the lowest). A win earns RP; 100 RP moves you up a division.
  • In division 1, reaching 100 RP starts a promotion series to the next tier: best of 3, or best of 5 into Master and Legendary.
  • Losing at 0 RP drops you a division (to 75 RP), but never out of a tier you've reached.
  • Master has no divisions. Legendary has no ceiling: the ladder sorts it by RP.
  • Beating a stronger player is worth a little more RP. Leaving a ranked game counts as a loss.

Unlocks & rewards

Every realm's base cards are free from the start. Winning matches unlocks Legendary cards, boards and expansion sets such as the Arsenal weapons. Unlocks are tied to your account, so they follow you to every device.

New realms are chosen by the community: cast your vote.
