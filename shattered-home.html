First time here?

  1. Make an account on the account page (it's separate from your Comic / Pokémon login) or right inside the game.
  2. Pick two realms and take the recommended 40-card deck. The realm guide lists each one's strengths.
  3. Play a Quick match, then move to Ranked once your deck feels right. The manual covers every rule and keyword.
