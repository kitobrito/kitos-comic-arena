The Alliance recruitment section lets you build a wishlist for your Alliance and send invitations to bring in new members.

The Alliance info section lets you customize key Alliance details and give your group a stronger identity.

The Alliance management section lets you organize members, create custom rank names, and assign them.

The Alliance avatar is the emblem for your group and appears anywhere your Alliance profile is shown.

The Alliance invitations section shows every invite you have received. Accept one to join that Alliance.

The Alliance register form lets you create a Alliance when you are currently not part of one.

You can always decide to leave your Alliance. If you leave as the Alliance Leader your Alliance will be disbanded.
